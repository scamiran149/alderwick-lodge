Life at Alderwick

Make yourself at home.

Unpack slowly. Follow the shore. There’s always a light on when you get back.

At the front desk

A key for the night.

The bell sticks in damp weather. If nobody comes, take a cabin key from the rail. Each one has a little brass tooth worn smooth by someone else’s hand.
